The Day I Was Born

Add baby photo
Add family photo

I was born on **30th July** as the third child in my family, with two loving elder brothers eagerly waiting to welcome me. I was the only princess of the family, and my parents were excited to see me for the first time. My name, **Surya Gandhi**, has a beautiful meaning. Surya is my aunt's name, and Gandhimathi was my grandmother's name. My family combined these names to give me this special identity. I love to think of myself as a sunflower, spreading happiness and brightness wherever I go. My birthday is also the day my grandmother passed away, making it a day filled with both happiness and memories. ❤️

“From the moment you arrived, our family was complete. You were our little princess, bringing double happiness and endless love into our lives."
